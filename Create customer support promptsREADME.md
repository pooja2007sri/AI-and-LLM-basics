## Overview
This folder documents my notes and learnings for **Create customer support prompts**, covering the fundamentals of how generative AI creates new content, common model types, practical use cases, and the importance of human oversight.

## Key Concepts & Best Practices
* **How Generative AI Works:** Produces new text, images, audio, and code by learning patterns from training data.
* **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Text-generating models such as Claude, GPT, Gemini, and Llama.
* **Image Generation Models:** Visual models such as Stable Diffusion, DALL·E, Midjourney, and Imagen.
* **Practical Applications:** Writing, coding, tutoring, design, and customer support workflows.
* **Human Review:** AI outputs can be inaccurate or fabricated, so human verification is always essential.

## Key Terms
* **Generative AI:** AI systems capable of creating brand-new content rather than just classifying or analyzing existing data.
* **LLM (Large Language Model):** A deep learning model trained on vast text data to understand and generate human-like language.
* **Diffusion Model:** An image generation model that creates visuals by iteratively denoising random noise.
* **Hallucination:** When an AI model generates confident-sounding but factually false or fabricated information.

## What I Learned
* Treating generative AI like a fast, creative apprentice—leveraging its speed for drafting customer support and text workflows while always checking the final work—prevents errors and hallucinations from reaching users.
