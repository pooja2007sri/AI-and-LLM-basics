# Create Social Media Content Prompts

## Goal
Build structured prompts for generating AI images, audio, and video content for social media while applying clear visual details, negative prompts, and ethical guidelines around copyright, consent, and disclosure.

## What I Did
- Crafted detailed image prompts specifying subject, style, lighting, composition, colours, and aspect ratio.
- Added negative prompts and style references to control unwanted elements and maintain consistent aesthetics across tools like Adobe Firefly, Midjourney, DALL·E, Stable Diffusion, and Ideogram.
- Explored prompt structures for AI audio and video workflows, including text-to-speech, music generation, and AI video tools.
- Reviewed every prompt against responsible AI practices—respecting copyright, consent, deepfake risks, and disclosure rules.
- Organized the prompt examples and notes inside a dedicated folder in my GitHub repository.

## How to Run or Read
1. Open the prompt files in this folder and copy a template into your preferred AI image, audio, or video generation tool.
2. Customize the subject, style, lighting, colour palette, aspect ratio, and negative prompt fields for your target social media platform.
3. Review the generated output to ensure it meets quality standards and follows copyright, consent, and AI disclosure guidelines.

## What I Learned
- Image prompting works just like describing a photo to an artist over the phone—specific details around lighting, composition, and aspect ratio make all the difference.
- Negative prompts are essential for filtering out visual artifacts and keeping outputs clean.
- Responsible content creation requires always respecting copyright, personal consent, and transparent AI disclosure.
